# Transport Hub Connect

You are an expert SaaS website designer, conversion-focused UX strategist, product marketer, UI designer and senior frontend developer.

Your task is to DESIGN AND BUILD a premium, modern, conversion-focused landing page for a SaaS product called:

LOGITRACK

Do not create a generic logistics website.

Do not create a generic fleet-management website.

Do not create a generic ERP website.

Build a focused SaaS marketing website for a transport business management platform designed around the real daily workflow of transport operators.

============================================================

1. PRODUCT CONTEXT

============================================================

Product name:

LogiTrack

Category:

Transport Business Management Software

Primary audience:

Truck owners and small fleet operators.

Secondary audience:

Larger transport companies.

There are two product offerings:

1. LogiTrack Lite

2. LogiTrack Pro

LogiTrack Lite is the primary entry product and is designed around simple everyday transport business operations.

LogiTrack Pro is designed for larger and more complex transport companies.

The website must make LogiTrack Lite feel like the right, purpose-built solution for everyday transport operations.

DO NOT make Lite look like a cheap or inferior version of Pro.

The positioning should be:

"Start with what your business needs today. Scale when you need to."

============================================================

2. CORE BRAND POSITIONING

============================================================

Primary brand idea:

"Your Trucks. Your Trips. Your Money. One Place."

Core promise:

"Run your transport business from one place."

Emotional/business outcome:

"Spend less time chasing information and more time running your business."

The fundamental problem LogiTrack solves:

Transport businesses often manage information across:

- WhatsApp

- Phone calls

- Excel

- Tally

- notebooks

- separate payment records

- personal memory

The problem is not that these tools are bad.

The problem is that the business information is scattered.

The owner/operator often becomes the person connecting all the information.

LogiTrack becomes the connected business record.

The conceptual flow is:

Booking

→ Vehicle & Driver

→ Trip

→ Expenses

→ Profit

→ Invoice

→ Customer Payment

Vehicles and drivers remain connected to the operational workflow.

============================================================

3. PRIMARY CUSTOMER PAINS

============================================================

The website should speak directly to these real business problems:

- Information is scattered across different places.

- Bookings come through WhatsApp and phone calls.

- Driver coordination happens separately.

- Trip expenses are difficult to consolidate.

- Owners struggle to understand what each trip actually made.

- Customer payment follow-up requires constant checking.

- Vehicle expenses are difficult to track over time.

- Maintenance and compliance information can be missed.

- Driver payments and outstanding amounts are difficult to reconcile.

- Owners need a consolidated view of the business.

- Too much of the business depends on the owner's memory and manual follow-up.

Do NOT make the customer feel incompetent.

Do NOT criticize WhatsApp, Excel or Tally.

Use an empathetic tone:

"These tools are useful individually, but your transport business needs the information connected."
